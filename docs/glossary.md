# Working Glossary

These definitions make the project's claims testable and may be revised as the
research develops.

## Surface-level pattern

A statistical relationship between words or phrases that allows a model to
predict an answer without consistently tracking the underlying state of the
fictional world.

## Internal representation

A pattern in the model's hidden states that reliably corresponds to a property
of the fictional world, such as an object's current carrier or location.

## Limited world model

An internal system that maintains information about agents, object carriers,
and object locations; updates that information when events occur; generalizes
the transition rules to unfamiliar stories; and uses the resulting state to
answer questions.

## Behavioral understanding

The ability to produce consistently appropriate answers. Behavioral success is
important, but it may also be produced by memorization or shortcuts.

## Representational understanding

The possession of structured internal states corresponding to objects,
properties, or relationships. Decodability alone does not show that a
representation is used.

## Functional understanding

The ability to use structured internal representations systematically in
reasoning, prediction, or action. This project treats causal use as stronger
evidence than behavioral success or decodability alone.

## Human-like semantic understanding

Understanding connected to a broad network of concepts, perception, action,
communication, and lived experience. The fictional-world experiment cannot
establish this.

## Phenomenal consciousness

The existence of subjective experience: there being something it feels like
to be the system. This project does not directly test phenomenal consciousness.

## Generalization

The ability to apply what was learned to examples that differ deliberately
from the training data, such as new names, unseen combinations, longer stories,
or unfamiliar wording.

## Linear probe

A simple classifier trained on frozen hidden states to test whether a property
can be decoded from a model's internal representation.

## Causal intervention

A controlled change to part of the model's computation used to test whether a
suspected internal representation actually influences the output.
